The five steps

How to actually begin.

1

Pick your niche & your lane

Choose what you'll post about and how you'll earn — creator, manager, or both. Clarity beats talent.

2

Post with a system, not by mood

Hooks, formats and a simple weekly plan. Consistency is the whole game — the system does the heavy lifting.

3

Grow your audience

Learn what makes people stop scrolling. One video can change your life — I've seen 18M+ likes on a single post.

4

Monetise — early

Land your first brand deal or client. You don't need millions of followers to get paid; you need the right approach.

5

Scale to full-time
